# Code of conduct

Open Loops welcomes respectful participation across backgrounds, identities and experience levels. This policy applies to the project's issues, pull requests and other project-managed spaces.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss the work without attacking the person.
- Give specific feedback and respect differing experience levels.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate or post sexualized material.
- Do not publish another person's private information without consent.
- Respect boundaries and stop unwanted contact when asked.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation or escalate abuse to GitHub. Moderation should consider context and avoid exposing private report details.

## Reporting

Named conduct contacts and a private project reporting channel are **TBD**. Do not publish private details in a public issue. GitHub's [reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) are available for platform abuse. Use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for software vulnerabilities.

The remaining contact and escalation decisions are tracked in [OSS readiness](docs/OSS_READINESS.md). This is a project-specific policy, not a claim that a separate external enforcement service exists.
