What these numbers mean

Accepted: unique quest IDs with an explicit acceptance event. Each gets 10 XP once, even if the job later fails.

Completed: unique agent jobs with a result-ready event. A prepared draft is not a sent message or source change.

Verified closure: a source readback confirms open → closed after separate approval. “Stale” means the request was open for at least 30 days. All receipts here are fixtures.

Decision time: question submitted → explicit decision confirmed, including evidence wait. Median of matched pairs in this example day. No baseline or measured improvement is claimed.
